Forerun — Privacy

Last updated 9 August 2026

Forerun collects nothing. There is no account, no sign-up, no analytics, no advertising, and no server that belongs to us. Everything the app knows stays on your iPhone.

What stays on your device

  • The events you chose to track, and the prep plans built from them.
  • Any notes, links or photos you attach to an event.
  • References to people you picked for an event — an identifier and a display name only. Phone numbers and email addresses are never stored; they are read from Contacts at the moment you tap Message and are not kept afterwards.
  • Your settings.

This data is stored in the app's own container. Deleting Forerun deletes all of it. You can also erase everything from Settings → Your data → Delete all data, and export a readable copy at any time.

Calendar

Forerun reads the calendars you select so it can see what is coming. It writes to your calendar in exactly one case: when you tap "Block the time" on a deep-work step, which creates a single event you can delete like any other. It never edits or deletes anything else.

Notifications

Reminders are scheduled locally by your iPhone. There are no push notifications, so nothing about your schedule is sent anywhere in order to remind you.

Apple Intelligence

When your device supports it, Forerun uses Apple's on-device model to reword a reminder in plainer language. That processing happens on the device. If the model is unavailable, Forerun uses its own written text and works exactly the same.

TickTick

If — and only if — you connect a TickTick account, Forerun reads your task lists directly from TickTick using a token stored in your device's Keychain. That connection is between your iPhone and TickTick; it does not pass through any server of ours, because we do not operate one. You can disconnect at any time in Settings, which deletes the token.

Children

Forerun is not directed at children and collects no personal information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the date at the top changes with it.
